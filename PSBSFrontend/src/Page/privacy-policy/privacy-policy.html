Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share your personal information when you use our Photography Service Booking System. By using our platform, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Information We Collect
  • Personal details (Name, Email, Phone Number)
  • Account credentials (Login information)
  • Booking details (Date, time, package, photographer)
  • Payment information (Processed securely via third-party gateways)
  • Reviews, ratings, and feedback
2. How We Use Your Information
  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To process bookings and payments

3. Photographer & Client Data

Photographers can access only necessary client details required to complete a booking. Clients may view photographer profiles, portfolios, and reviews. Data is never sold or misused.

4. Payment Security

All payments are handled through secure payment gateways such as bKash, Nagad, or Card services. We do not store sensitive payment credentials on our servers.

5. Data Protection

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Cookies may be used to enhance user experience, remember preferences, and analyze system usage.

7. User Rights

  • Opt out of promotional notifications
8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the platform indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team.
